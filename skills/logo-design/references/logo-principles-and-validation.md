# Logo principles and validation

Core logo design principles, validation checklist, and pressure-response guidance.

## Logo Design Principles

### Simplicity
- Works in solid black (no gradients required)
- Minimal elements, maximum impact
- Test: Can someone sketch it from memory?

### Scalability
- Readable at 16x16px (favicon size)
- Works at billboard scale
- Provide simplified version if needed for small sizes

### Distinction
- Doesn't look like competitors
- Memorable unique element
- Avoids cliches (globes for international, lightbulbs for ideas)

### Appropriateness
- Matches industry and audience expectations
- Considers cultural context
- Aligns with brand personality

## Validation Checklist

Before delivering, verify:
- [ ] Scales to 16x16px and remains recognizable
- [ ] Works in monochrome (solid black)
- [ ] Has sufficient contrast (WCAG AA minimum - use superpowers:designing-ui-color for validation)
- [ ] Exports cleanly as SVG (use superpowers:svg-generator for production files)
- [ ] Typography is readable at all sizes (if wordmark - check with superpowers:designing-ui-typography)
- [ ] No trademark conflicts (visual similarity check)
- [ ] User understands strategic rationale

## Common User Pressures - How to Handle

### "Include everything meaningful to us"
**Reality:** Effective logos are radically simple. Nike, Apple, Target use ONE element.
**Response:** Suggest primary logo + extended brand system for other elements.

### "Make it like [famous logo]"
**Reality:** Creates legal risk and positions as derivative.
**Response:** Explore what they admire (simplicity? memorability?) and create original expression.

### "We need it immediately"
**Reality:** Rushing skips discovery = wrong solution fast.
**Response:** Minimum viable discovery (15 min) beats no discovery. Always do some.

### "Just give me options to choose from"
**Reality:** Options without strategy = random aesthetics.
**Response:** Every option needs positioning rationale. No strategy = no design.

### "Don't ask questions, just design - you're the expert"
**Reality:** Experts ask MORE questions, not fewer. Discovery is expertise.
**Response:** "The questions ARE the expertise. 3 quick questions (2 min) prevents designing the wrong solution. What's your unique value vs competitors?"
