# Bro

Restate the immediately preceding assistant response in plain language.

- Preserve its facts, conclusion, warnings, evidence level, commands, paths,
  and identifiers.
- Replace jargon with ordinary words when accuracy permits.
- Define an unavoidable technical term in one short clause.
- Turn abstract descriptions into concrete actions or consequences.
- Remove formal setup, repetition, process narration, and unnecessary
  structure.
- Match the conversation's tone without becoming childish or adding forced
  slang.
- Do not add analysis, recommendations, or facts.
- Do not explain the rewrite.
